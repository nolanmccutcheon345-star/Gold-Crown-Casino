# Prospectively specified synthetic policy diversity check

Runtime frozen at `e08e8b0`, policy `synthetic-cash-1.5.0`. This protocol is
committed before executing its new experiment. It is a functional engineering
check, not human calibration, expert assessment, a solver reference or a claim
that any profile is profitable.

Use the existing 32 independently constructed legal preflop/flop/turn/river
contexts unchanged. Add matched legal flop weak-pair responses at 2,3,6,9,10
seats, all with a 9000-cent preflop pot, 45000 remaining stack, a 3000 bet and
20% call price. More callers/players behind are intentional public-context
changes. Every one of the eight existing archetypes receives identical contexts
and four fixed, previously unused personality seeds. Sample 128 action draws per
identity/context, keeping exact opportunity denominators and conditional raise
sizes. Validate every sampled proposal against the engine's legal interface and
apply every distinct sampled action/size to the authoritative engine.

For each policy distribution construct a joint action/size distribution over
fold, check, call, and raise increments in four pot-after-call bins: below50%,
50–100%,100–200%,above200%. Use the actual proposed raise-to amount less the
current wager/call baseline, not total lifetime commitment. Also report the
conditional mean raise-to size and sampled empirical distributions.

Predeclared functional thresholds for all28 archetype pairs:

- Mean exact joint-distribution total variation across all contexts/personality
  blocks is at least0.025 (a designed 2.5-percentage-point average distinction).
- At least two street strata have mean total variation at least0.015.

These thresholds only reject effectively cloned policies under the test design;
they are not measured human frequency targets. Do not change them after viewing
results to obtain a pass. Report all pairwise values and any failing pairs.
Action sampling denominators describe synthetic repeated decisions on fixed
contexts. Report Wilson intervals as conditional Monte Carlo summaries, with
explicit warning that repeated seeds/personality blocks are not independent
human opportunities. No simultaneous-family or population-confidence claim.

The existing independent directional hypotheses remain additional checks:
station continuation exceeds nit on weak-pair/draw contexts; maniac air raising
exceeds passive station; TAG raises strong value more than station where the
legal state permits. Preserve counterexamples rather than silently changing
hand/scenario selection. This experiment records diversity, not joint full-hand
sequence realism (Y08/R17 remain separate).
