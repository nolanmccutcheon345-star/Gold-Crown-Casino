# Step4C independent evaluation protocol — registered before candidate tuning

Source baseline: published Version6 synthetic-cash-1.1.0 frozen under src/bots/v2. Candidate synthetic-cash-1.2.0. No policy-specific rate thresholds and no claim of human calibration. Production policies are black boxes for observed rate calculation: only returned legal actions, public events and test-only independently enumerated visible hand properties are classified. True opponent profiles/cards never enter observations. Engine mechanics remain authoritative; financial outcomes are separately reconciled from events.

Population plan: 72 distinct table/personality blocks = 2/6/9/10 seats ×20/100/250BB ×low/mid/high synthetic environment ×2 lineup replications. Each block has20 full hands. Replication0 rotates all eight styles; replication1 uses population-random identities with Unknown and Custom seats included. Skill assignments rotate .3/.65/.9 independently of stake environment. Rake alternates zero and5% capped2.5BB, no-flop-no-drop. All seats play; none is treated as Hero. Each controlled trial resets starting stacks to the specified depth (one seat half depth every fifth trial to produce heterogeneous effective stacks/side pots); identities and legitimate public memory remain stable across the20-trial block. This is a cluster of completed-hand experiments, not a live bankroll session or representative human sample. Models share cards, profile seeds and per-decision action streams but divergent histories naturally diverge opportunity counts.

Development population seed family0x4c110000. Fresh candidate evaluation population seed family0x4c910000; no results are inspected by policy authors before their source is frozen. The heldout cohort is consumed if used for fixes; any subsequent claim needs a fresh seed namespace and recorded reason. Deck randomness, personality randomness and action randomness are independent.

Probe plan: eight independently authored strategies (quarter-pot pressure, polarized river-only overbet, passive thin value, missed-draw selective bluff, never-fold, stack-pressure, small-probe-then-shove, multiway squeeze) ×eight evaluated styles ×four independent blocks ×12 hands, mirroring focus/button roles. Baseline/candidate first use development block2×8 to reveal mechanical defects; final heldout block8×8 uses new seeds and25/75/175/300BB depths. Test probes receive only private/public observations and never policy diagnostics. Outcome intervals resample full blocks, not individual decisions; eight-block intervals remain sparse and are explicitly insufficient for profitability/exploitability claims.

Metrics: each observed hand/seat defines VPIP opportunity if a voluntary decision occurred; PFR additionally requires at least one legal raise. RFI is an actor decision before any voluntary call/raise with legal raising, excluding BB walk/free option; physical position labels are independently computed at every supported table size, with UTG+1 etc retained rather than collapsed. Three/four-bet opportunities require exactly1/2 prior preflop raises, legal raising and the actor not being the latest raiser. Fold-to-three-bet is the opener's first faced response after the second raise. Cold call means no prior voluntary contribution facing an open and excludes blinds. Squeeze requires an open plus cold call and no intervening raise. Blind defense is the SB/BB's first priced response to an open. Postflop cbet requires last preflop raiser, unopened flop and legal bet; barrels require same actor's bet on prior street and unopened current street. Check-raise requires earlier check followed by facing a bet with legal raising, once per street. Fold-to-cbet follows a true recorded cbet, first response per seat. WTSD = showdown participant / saw flop; W$SD = positive payout / showdown participant (ties count as wins; separately labeled); WWSF = positive payout / saw flop. All-in runouts count as flop seen when eligible at board appearance.

Hand categories and draws use a separate audit implementation, never bot features/ranges: exact evaluator category plus private contribution; flush draws need four same-suit cards with private contribution; straight draws require one-card completion with private rank contribution. Draw metrics exclude river and already-made straights/flushes. 'Value proxy' is two-pair-or-better involving private cards or top pair, 'air bet proxy' is no pair/no current draw with private non-board contribution. They are operational diagnostics, not true range EV or proof of bluff intent. River bluff-catch proxy is one private pair facing a bet. Showdown/nonshowdown net is per-seat result partitioned by hand ending, not adjusted EV.

Uncertainty: key aggregate rates use deterministic percentile bootstrap of whole table/personality blocks (1000resamples); null if fewer than8 contributing blocks. Block counts and binomial Wilson references also shown. Wilson is a conditional Monte Carlo reference that does not account for clustering. Rare rates explicitly report insufficient coverage when fewer than100 opportunities or8 blocks; this is a precision disclosure, not a strategy pass threshold. Outcomes use the same cluster bootstrap; heavy tails and scarce rare premiums remain limitations. No mathematical exploitability bound, independent-expert endorsement, human-frequency resemblance or real-money profitability is inferred.

All complete hand event traces, initial seeds/profiles, classifications, exact opportunities, latency, engine-free payout reconciliation, and representative review packets are retained. Expert packet status is NOT_REVIEWED. Required falsifiable tests: counter arithmetic, full52-card uniqueness, legal actions, exact public-observation secret counterfactual equality, seat/role independence, posterior normalization/card removal, completed-hand conservation, and no evaluator code in production imports.

Audit correction before final cohort: freeze observed bet/raise increment divided by pot after the raiser called at its event; later callers cannot change whether the same wager was small/oversized. The first development population run used a changing pot denominator, was discarded for those metrics, and is rerun. A no-flush-draw fixture initially held As5s on Kh7h2h3h (actually wheel gutshot); corrected to AsQs before any population run. Neither change changes strategy targets.
