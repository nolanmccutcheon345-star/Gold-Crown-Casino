# Historical 10.072-second preparation: present risk assessment

**UNATTRIBUTED; not fixed.** The original observation does not retain the full decision timestamp, contemporaneous CPU/GC trace and every phase boundary needed to reconstruct its cause. There is no defensible observed recurrence rate without its denominator and comparable sampling conditions.

The preceding Step5G evidence includes12,011 preparation samples in1,000-history recovery stress (maximum291.80ms), and1,002 samples in a10,000-history uninterrupted natural-GC profile (maximum470.19ms). The first scenario deliberately forces GC and injects reloads; the second uses Inspector sampling and a disk request reference. These are not identical independent samples and do not establish an IID probability bound for native browsers. Another4,008-span attempt retained a trace with maximum797.06ms but failed final profile-artifact capture; its incomplete evidence remains marked FAIL. None explains the old10.072-second observation.

A sufficiently long synchronous stall could prevent betting controls, announcements and paints from responding, provoke repeated input, or appear to be lost progress. A long pending asynchronous preparation could delay the bot while leaving some UI responsive. Those consequences differ; only an actual matching trace can decide. The native Step5H harness separates preparation from initial conversion, archive auditing, inventory serialization, render readiness and browser task/frame timestamps. GC attribution is explicitly unavailable in standard browser APIs. No hypothetical attribution is reported as a measured cause.

Observed protections reduce accounting consequences, not the inconvenience of a stall. Production prepares one durable decision identity and token before action, commits through revision/ownership checks and preserves the last committed state if persistence fails. The preceding48 pending-reload checks retained decision identity and accounting; those are controlled recovery evidence, not proof of what happened during the historical outlier. Step5H actual browser receipts must independently record current recovery and duplicate-action checks before they are cited.

Release risk remains open for U11/V518 device performance and Z14's complete bounded-storage/performance conjunction. The test runner captures the latest measured phases and retains timestamps/timing totals when a run fails. A developer can reproduce the workload and preserve downloaded receipts without public telemetry or hidden-card diagnostics in ordinary play. If the tail recurs, retain the exact source/runtime manifest, input population, phase windows, long-task overlaps, native storage usage, memory availability and pending revision/decision identity. Do not erase the occurrence, increase artificial think-time, use arbitrary cancellation, or skip integrity checks to hide it.

The engineering decision is conditional: preserve existing safeguards and the unresolved finding; continue actual native-browser/device sampling and investigate reproducible phase costs. Absence in the new bounded samples is evidence of those samples only. It is not release approval or proof that a rare stall cannot recur.
