3. Stratified reference matrix: enumerate every legal opposing two-card combination, then every legal river for turn fixtures. No production range sampler, hand-feature heuristic or pot-pricing function is used by reference equity. River terminal classes: private nuts, set, top two pair, QQ bluff-catcher, weak high-card, ace-flush-blocker and no-blocker; turn draw and weak draw. Value bucket=two-pair-or-better OR pair matching highest board rank; bluff bucket=high card. Excluded middle-strength combos are a deliberate polarized-range assumption, not an empirical claim.

Unknown holdings remain unknown to bot; this is a sensitivity audit, not a demand that it clairvoyantly match the selected truth range.
5. Public-memory conditions derive ONLY from complete engine-committed fixture histories (passive/value-heavy, mixed, frequent aggressor); expose no private actual reference-range label to bot. Report full histories and observation counts. Range and personality priors are synthetic, not calibrated human observations.
6. For each case/memory cell sample1000 independent seeded decision tokens through the actual policy. Denominator is identical conditional decision opportunities, not hands/VPIP. Record actual legal actions, Wilson95% conditional Monte Carlo interval, probabilities and all range assumptions. These samples do not establish independent strategic/human calibration.
7. Tests assert mechanics, exact EV arithmetic, card removal, distinguishable positive/negative/close reference calls, legal actions, historical compatibility and regression. Matrix frequencies are descriptive; no minimum continuation target. Any anomalous behavior becomes a limitation or separately evidenced defect, never rewritten expectations.
