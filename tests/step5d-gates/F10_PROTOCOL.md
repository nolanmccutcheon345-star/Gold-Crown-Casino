# Step5D held-out dangerous-river diagnostic — frozen before execution

This protocol extends, rather than replaces, Step5C's eight board/hand clusters. No production policy, seed, utility assumption or acceptance threshold is tuned after outcomes. Twelve new board families each contain two value holdings (24 board/hand clusters), tested at40BB and150BB effective starting stacks, checked-to in position and facing33%,150% and300% pot. The exact cards are in `river-heldout.test.ts`. All public action prefixes execute through the authoritative engine: open to5BB, call, flop checks, turn checks, then the river check/bet. No private opponent cards or deck enter the bot observation.

Compare stronger-reg(skill0.9) with passive-reg(skill0.25), the existing low-stakes recreational archetype. Four **new** stable identity seeds per archetype;32 **new** paired decision seeds per identity and situation. 24clusters×2depths×4rivercontexts×2archetypes×4identities×32draws =49,152 legal decisions. Each legal action and its size are independently priced against all990 compatible opponent combinations using the already independently written exhaustive evaluator in `tests/step5c-gates/river-reference.ts` (no production evaluator/range imports).

Retain all three previous range/response models, without tuning: value-heavy, broad and recreational category weights; category/price-dependent calling, no opponent re-raises. Calls/folds use exact pot and future call cost, ties split; this deliberately limited one-node utility is not full decisionEV, solver verification, expert assessment or measured human calibration. Opponent priors are stipulated and not secretly injected into the bot. Starting stacks constrain legal actions and hypothetical call amounts. Equal-weight all24clusters×2depths×4contexts×3range cells. Each archetype's opportunities per situation are128 (fouridentities×32 draws). Report action counts, complete public observation/history, legal actions, exact cards, stack depth, range assumptions and each candidate action's modeled utility.

A positive aggregate mean alone cannot promote F10. Report every negative cell, breakdowns by made-hand class/price/stack/range, and a descriptive paired bootstrap over the12 board families (10,000 bootstrap samples, seed0x5d0bcafe). Stronger-than-weak average and lower regret are measured hypotheses, not forced assertions. If the average or interval fails to support the hypothesis, retain F10 NOT_EXECUTED/PARTIAL and identify contexts for independently justified investigation. This check can establish only behavior in the declared synthetic population; it cannot establish universal optimality. The actual requirement remains unchanged: “Strong reg adjusts size/line more accurately on average; avoid unconditional \"always best\" claims”.

Commit this protocol and executable fixture before running results. Capture the executing source commit, file hashes, all seeds, versions, runtime, exact expected/observed assertions and raw outputs. No current source hash is retroactively assigned to an earlier run.
