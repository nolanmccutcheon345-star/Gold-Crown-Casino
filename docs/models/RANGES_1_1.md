# Public-action range model 1.1

`src/bots/ranges.ts` implements `public-action-ranges-1.1.0`. All numeric likelihoods are provisional synthetic assumptions. They are not human calibration, Bayesian posterior truth, solved ranges or decision EV.

Every possible two-card combination starts with equal dealt-card probability. Known own and community cards are removed. Position, stack depth, number of players, prior aggression and the observed bet price condition likelihoods of subsequent public actions. Public VPIP/PFR/aggression/fold observations are shrunk toward the configured population prior using `n/(n+24)` times the observer's adaptation trait. No actual other bot profile is read. VPIP and PFR memory retain the existing public acted-hand proxy denominator; they are not the canonical player statistics.

Each public action multiplies compatible combination weights by a designed likelihood. Before use, each hypothetical hand's action weights normalize to one across the reconstructed facing-bet (fold/call/raise) or free-check (fold/check/raise) family. This is a public generative approximation, not another engine for stack or reopening legality; the observed action is already engine-validated. Tiny free-check fold mass represents errors rather than an optimal action. Likelihood temperature is `.82 + .12 * public-confidence`. After normalization, a 2.5–6% uniform contamination component preserves unmodeled errors, traps and bluff hypotheses. Raising, calling and checking produce different distributions. A free big-blind check does not imply a voluntary preflop call. Prior-street likelihoods use only that street's board prefix. Newly public cards legitimately block hypotheses that have become impossible. Duplicate event IDs are ignored. Historical active-player counts are reconstructed before each action, so later folds do not reinterpret a multiway bet as heads-up. Folded players leave the active showdown comparison; their final inferred posteriors remain optional diagnostics, and their undisclosed cards do not become blockers.

Preflop likelihoods use the explicit 169-class model's designed ordinal strength score and speculative/blocker metadata, not estimated preflop equity. Postflop likelihood features approximate made-hand class, private contribution, draw quality and relevant suit blockers. Full combo distributions and action update traces are diagnostics only, never ordinary Live Table data.

Showdown equity samples 96 compatible joint tuples heads-up, 72 against two or three opponents, and 48 against four or more. Entire independent product draws are rejected on any card collision; sampling each opponent conditional on earlier selections would bias seating order. Uniform future runouts follow compatible tuple selection. The authoritative engine evaluator determines exact winners and ties for each sample. A finite rejection limit returns fewer samples with an explicit warning and high uncertainty; it never reads actual hidden cards. Reported standard error describes Monte Carlo variance only, not model misspecification.

Equity means sampled showdown share including ties. It excludes rake, side-pot eligibility, folds and future betting consequences. Postflop policy must separately consider price, rake, realization and future cost. The public-line range advantage compares posterior mean heuristic strength; nut advantage compares strong-tail mass, not exact mathematical nuts. Fold equity is a synthetic half-pot probe estimate with independent opponent responses. All-in opponents force fold equity to zero.

The advisory sampling stream is deterministic from permitted semantic observations. It does not consume action entropy or seed from raw hand, decision or seat identifiers. A maximum of eight deeply frozen analyses and four board-feature arrays may be cached; cache eviction changes performance only. Old policy versions remain dispatched to frozen code separately.

Known limitations: no solved strategy likelihoods; no empirical range calibration; independent range factors before card-removal conditioning; independent fold responses; no side-pot-specific equity; no likelihood fitting from shown hands; coarse high-tail and range-advantage proxies; bounded-sample noise. Tests establish privacy, consistency, action conditioning, accounting preservation and reproducibility, not human realism.
