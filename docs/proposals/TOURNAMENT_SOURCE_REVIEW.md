# Tournament brief: source review at Version31 plus patch15

The supplied brief is preserved unchanged in SUPPLIED_TOURNAMENT_BRIEF.md as an external proposal, not a replacement for the302 original criteria or86 future features. Patch15 implements branding only. No tournament or blackjack functionality is claimed here.

1. Forced bets: current RuleProfile explicitly permits only cash mode. core/engine.ts validates that restriction and posts two blinds. There is no tournament BB-ante/dead-button profile to enable. A versioned tournament rules extension and independent fixtures are required; it must preserve historical cash profiles and monetary semantics.
2. Persistence: AppData is schemaVersion4 with one cash wallet. A future separate tournament namespace should have its own event identities, balance adjustments, entry fees and prizes, not cash pokerNet or BB/100. The exact schema/migration remains to be implemented and tested with populated backups and rollback. A type-level tournament-payout label in an older contract is not an implemented tournament ledger.
3. Workers: chooseDecision consumes a permitted observation, profile and injected randomness. SessionController.prepareBot is NOT a portable worker job as written: it commits a draw token, builds public memory from the active session/archive and persists the decision through mutate. A worker boundary should take a frozen, redacted observation plus policy version and deterministic token; the owning controller must validate returned identity/revision and retain commit authority. Worker compatibility and measured throughput remain untested.
4. Round recovery: synchronized rounds can be designed to fit the checkpoint/journal pattern, but today's recovery contract binds one active cash table. An MTT needs an explicit durable round transaction with per-table revisions, deterministic seed streams, pending work, eliminations and exactly-once payouts. Atomic multi-table recovery does not exist merely because rounds are synchronized.

Seed-only background histories are not yet demonstrated sufficient for audit/replay. Reproduction also depends on frozen policy/engine/rules versions, personality and public-memory inputs, decision randomness and seating transitions; record and verify those inputs or retain canonical actions. Do not omit required records based only on estimated storage savings.

The proposed rule-year label, payouts, round schedule, field sizes and cost estimates have not been independently qualified in this change. The current R1 cash requirements remain unchanged. MTT and blackjack work is not part of this branding release; no deferred IDs or statuses were rewritten.
